# Release Note: the KASH convert sheet no longer shows the redemption reserve

## What changed

"Convert to USD" showed the desk's public reserve under the user's payout.
A four-figure sum beneath a seven-dollar payout read as a fee, a lock, or a
mistake. The line is gone; the payout stays. The desk still refuses a payout
its reserve cannot cover, and that message is unchanged.
