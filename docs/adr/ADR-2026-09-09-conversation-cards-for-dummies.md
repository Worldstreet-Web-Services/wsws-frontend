# ADR-2026-09-09: Four more cards under "Join the Conversation" (Plain English Guide / ADR for Dummies)

## Status

Proposed, 2026-09-09. Waiting for the maintainer to authorize.

## The Problem

The dashboard has a sliding band called "Join the Conversation". It was
built for two cards and now has one, the chess room, so it does not slide.

## What We Do

Add four cards in the same shape and size, each with its own colour and
picture: Last Man Standing in ember red with a countdown ring, Checkers in
green with a board corner, ArkBall in amber with its own artwork, and Market
Square in teal with faces of people in a live room. Each card has the same
two buttons as the chess card: one to jump in, one to learn more.

Where the site already knows something live, the card says so: the pot and
seconds left on a Last Man round, how many checkers matches are on, which
room is live on Market Square. When nothing is live, the card invites you in
instead of showing an error.

## What Changes For Users

The band slides through five cards. Market Square links open in a new tab.

## What Does Not Change

The chess card looks exactly as it does now. No game or page is added or
removed; every button leads to a page that already exists.
