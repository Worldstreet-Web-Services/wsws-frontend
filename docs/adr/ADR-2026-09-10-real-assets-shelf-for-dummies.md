# The "Own the Real World" shelf, in plain English

## What is happening

The home screen has rows of cards that lead into each part of the app:
token moves, conversations, memecoins, predictions. Real assets, which are
tokenised gold, treasuries, stocks and property, have no row.

## What we are doing

We are adding a fifth row at the bottom, "Own the Real World", with four
cards: Gold, Treasuries, Real estate and Stocks. Each shows a real asset from
that group with its live price or yield, and a button into the Real assets
desk. The cards are drawn to look like what they are, bars of gold, a yield
figure, a skyline, a ticker tape, and not like the memecoin cards.

Only people who said they were interested in stocks, gold, yield, real estate
or treasuries when they first signed up will see the row. Everyone else's home
screen stays as it is.

## Why it matters

Someone who told us they came for gold or treasuries should meet those on
the home screen, not have to find the desk. And the row costs the app no
extra network traffic: it reads the same feed the home screen already loads.
