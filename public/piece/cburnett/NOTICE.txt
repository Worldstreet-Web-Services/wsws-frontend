"Cburnett" chess piece images.

By Colin M.L. Burnett, the classic set used by Wikipedia and lichess.
Triple-licensed: GPLv2+, BSD, and CC-BY-SA 3.0 — free to use with this
attribution. PNGs are Wikimedia Commons' own 150px renders of the SVGs
(commons.wikimedia.org, files Chess_<piece><l|d>t45.svg).

The board and captured-piece rows build their URLs as
/piece/cburnett/<c><t>.png (c = w|b, t = p|n|b|r|q|k). To swap sets, drop
another licensed set into a sibling folder under the same filenames and
update the one path constant in chess-board.tsx / captured-row.tsx.
