# Release Note: the 2.0 interface, minus what production cannot serve

## What changed

The whole of staging's 2.0 interface is merged onto main and five sections
are removed from it, because they need backends the production gateway
does not serve or are not wanted yet:

- Perpetuals, entirely: no `/perps` route, no desk, no brief, no nav entry.
- Real assets, entirely: no `/rwa` route, no desk, no brief, no nav entry.
  A token already held still shows in the portfolio and is priced; its
  sheet offers no trade.
- The Explore prediction market, the sportsbook, the combos desk and the
  `/market` page. The prediction desk keeps its 2.0 look; its Polymarket
  cards are unlinked as they were on main.
- Arkjet and Pilot Chicken. The Arkade keeps ArkBall, Chess, Checkers and
  Last Man Standing.
- The three design-review preview routes.

The `/dashboard` to `/portfolio` redirect ships temporary for this release.
The bottom tab bar's second icon opens the spot desk.
